Why we're asking
PWA is building its first formal strategic plan, and no one has a better read on whether the training actually held up in the field than the people who've been through it. Your experience since graduating — what prepared you, what didn't, and what the field actually demands — will directly shape our goals for curriculum, certification support, and future programs.

A note on answering
Be candid. Critical feedback is at least as useful as praise — we're trying to see the program clearly, not collect compliments.

Takes about 8–10 minutes
